# Approved design contract

## Purpose

Plexigent's website creates allure and intrigue. Paul explicitly rejected the earlier proposal to lead with service explanations, case studies, credentials, and conversion-oriented consulting copy. The approved experience is an invitation to discover.

Every stable visible composition must be clean, elegant, restrained, beautiful, crisp, and resonant. Motion carries the visitor between compositions and resolves into near stillness. The Apple-reveal reference concerns composition, pacing, and restraint, not copying Apple's assets or layout.

## Two enduring brand symbols

1. **xi:** Latin lowercase x followed by lowercase i. The forward-leaning `/` stroke of x and the dot of i are cyan. The other stroke and the i stem remain light neutral. Preserve the accent within the full Plexigent wordmark as well as the standalone mark. Do not substitute a Greek xi, recolor the entire x, or improvise a different diagonal.
2. **Connected-dot butterfly:** Preserve the included node positions, topology, and line geometry as the recognizable icon. The network and the butterfly represent elegant eureka, emerging from gathered complexity. The ambition is enduring independent recognition of both symbols.

The reference's moniker uses Arial glyphs plus CSS masks. Production may formalize this as matching vector master artwork to avoid platform-dependent glyph/mask drift. Match the approved shape, color, relative proportions, and spacing; do not introduce a new logo design under the guise of refactoring.

## Opening thesis, without explanatory words

Scattered dots become a caterpillar that consumes additional signals. Those signals reorganize within a chrysalis. The connected-dot butterfly emerges as elegant eureka. This is the core Plexigent operating model, not disposable decoration.

Keep that sequence on the main page, seeded on the first visible visit. Preserve recognizable consumption and metamorphosis moments, the graceful emergence, and the final stable butterfly/wordmark composition. Do not replace it with a generic particle loop, stock butterfly, video-only approximation, or text explaining the phases. Accessible descriptions may remain available to assistive technology.

The header xi and menu remain available throughout. After resolution, a quiet invitation leads into the second level. Replay restarts the sequence. Returning home within the experience holds the final composition after the opening has completed; replay is deliberate.

## Navigation and composition

- A persistent hamburger menu directly exposes all four full domain names, About Us, and Contact. No visitor must finish the opening or traverse every domain to reach a destination.
- Click/tap and upward swipe naturally advance the experience; backward movement is available. Preserve ordinary keyboard access.
- Each domain has a distinctive quiet dot composition, restrained type, one provocation, and one invitation. Preserve the full domain identity, even when “Plexigent” is a small separate prefix.
- Avoid permanent explanatory panels, service-card grids, dashboards, metrics, autoplay carousels, long copy, sound, or continuous decorative animation.
- Real production deep links, browser Back/Forward, focus handling, and safe-area behavior should be completed as part of implementation. Their absence in the study is not a design preference.
